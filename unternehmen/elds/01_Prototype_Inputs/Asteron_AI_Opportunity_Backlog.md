AI opportunity backlog
Entirely fictional. Case snapshot: August 31, 2026. Synthetic organizational evidence for a hackathon prototype.
Reading the backlog
Proposals as of August 31, 2026. Estimates are synthetic sponsor assumptions, not approved
business cases or measured returns. EUR 45/hour is a planning value for capacity, not the KPI
cost allocation rate. Build cost is one-time; running cost is monthly. Benefits must not be added
when tasks or saved minutes overlap.
AI-01 | Ticket summarization
Origin: Support Operations. Expected benefit: Reduce wrap-up writing.
Estimated complexity: Low; 2-week sandbox build.
Assumed value: July pilot: 12 agents, 240 tickets, self-reported 2.5 minutes saved per eligible
ticket after checking. Sponsor extrapolates 18,000 x 2.5 / 60 = 750 hours/month, valued at EUR
45/hour = EUR 33,750 capacity/month. Assumes all tickets qualify. Build EUR 18,000; running
EUR 2,000/month.
Current status: Pilot completed; finance validation pending.
AI-02 | Automated classification
Origin: Service Desk lead. Expected benefit: Reduce manual labeling.
Estimated complexity: Low; 2-3 weeks.
Assumed value: 1 minute on 18,000 tickets = 300 hours/month; EUR 13,500 at EUR 45/hour.
Build EUR 15,000; running EUR 1,200/month. Offline label agreement 89% on 400 manually
labeled cases.
Current status: Sandbox experiment requested.
AI-03 | Intelligent routing
Origin: Regional leads. Expected benefit: Reduce incorrect first assignment.
Estimated complexity: Medium; 4-6 weeks.
Assumed value: Assumes 8% of tickets avoid 10 minutes of handling: 240 hours/month; EUR
10,800 capacity. Build EUR 30,000; running EUR 2,500/month. Requires fresher entitlement and
availability data.
Current status: Requirements discussion.
